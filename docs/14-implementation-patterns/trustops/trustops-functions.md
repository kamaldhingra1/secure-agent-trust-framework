# The Seven Functions of TrustOps

TrustOps operates through seven functions.

## 1. Establish Trust

Verify that the mission satisfies defined trust conditions before execution. This includes mission scope, agent identity, authority, approved tools, required governance policies, and evidence requirements.

## 2. Observe Trust

Collect trust telemetry during execution. This includes delegation events, model health, knowledge freshness, policy evaluations, tool invocations, runtime behavior, and outcome signals.

## 3. Evaluate Trust

Correlate evidence across domains to produce Trust Intelligence. Identify trust decay caused by stale evidence, changing context, evolving threats, or accumulated uncertainty.

## 4. Govern Trust

Translate confidence assessments into operational decisions. A mission may continue, continue with constraints, require human oversight, pause, or terminate.

## 5. Recover Trust

Restore confidence through recovery actions such as refreshing knowledge sources, replacing models, revoking delegation, requiring approval, rotating credentials, or rerouting through trusted services.

## 6. Learn Trust

Capture Trust Decision Records, governance outcomes, lessons learned, and operational observations to improve future trust policies and mission design.

## 7. Measure Trust

Measure trust as an operational discipline through defined metrics such as Mission Trust Index, Trust Decision Latency, Trust Recovery Time, Evidence Freshness, Delegation Integrity, and Secure Outcome Success Rate.

## Mapping to SATF

| TrustOps Function | SATF Alignment |
|---|---|
| Establish Trust | Ring 1: Trust Establishment |
| Observe Trust | Telemetry, Runtime Plane |
| Evaluate Trust | Agent Trust Fabric |
| Govern Trust | Governance, Telemetry, and Assurance Control Plane |
| Recover Trust | Runtime and Response Plane |
| Learn Trust | Assurance and continuous improvement |
| Measure Trust | Governance metrics and evidence reporting |

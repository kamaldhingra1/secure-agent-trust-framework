## Purpose

This map connects each threat domain to the Secure Outcomes that may be undermined.

The Out-of-Bound Agent domain can impact all five SATF secure outcomes:

- Safe Outcomes
- Trusted Outcomes
- Compliant Outcomes
- Resilient Outcomes
- Expected Outcomes
